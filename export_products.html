Common Questions

Export FAQs

Our standard MOQ for groundnut oil is 1 MT (metric tonne). For raw peanuts it's 5 MT, and for peanut butter it's 500 kg. For consolidated or mixed container loads, we can discuss combined MOQs. Contact our export team for specific requirements.
We primarily ship from Mundra Port (Gujarat) and Jawaharlal Nehru Port Trust (JNPT, Mumbai). Both support FCL and LCL shipments. We have established freight relationships with major shipping lines for competitive rates.
Yes. We offer full private label and OEM services for distributors and retail chains. We can produce bottles, cans, or flexible packaging with your brand artwork. Minimum order quantities apply. Share your design files and we'll handle everything from production to labelling.
We provide: Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Bill of Lading, Certificate of Origin (APEDA), Certificate of Analysis (COA), Phytosanitary Certificate, FSSAI Certificate, and any market-specific documents (e.g., EU Health Certificate, HALAL Certificate, US FDA registration). We can also arrange third-party inspections by SGS or Bureau Veritas upon request.
Absolutely. We offer free product samples (courier charges payable by the buyer) for qualified importers. Please mention your sample request in the inquiry form or contact us on WhatsApp. Sample dispatch typically happens within 5–7 working days.
Our High Oleic "Vedic" peanuts are developed through traditional selective breeding — non-GMO by nature. We are currently in the process of obtaining USDA NOP Organic certification. For specific organic requirements, please discuss with our export team. We source from farms that do not use prohibited pesticides and can provide pesticide residue reports.
