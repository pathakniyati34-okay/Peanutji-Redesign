Pure. Natural. Unrefined.

Peanutji Groundnut Oil is cold-extracted from carefully selected Grade A G-20 peanuts, sourced directly from the fertile farms of Saurashtra, Gujarat. Every batch passes through our proprietary 11-stage quality sorting process before reaching your kitchen.

Unlike refined oils that use hexane and bleaching chemicals, our oil retains its natural golden colour, earthy aroma, and full nutritional profile — Vitamin E, antioxidants, and healthy unsaturated fats — exactly as nature intended.

Our Process
1
Farm-to-Factory Sourcing
Direct procurement from 200+ contracted farmers in Rajkot, Junagadh, and Amreli districts of Gujarat.
2
11-Stage Quality Sorting
Mechanical grading, optical colour sorting, moisture testing, and aflatoxin screening before pressing.
3
Cold Mechanical Pressing
Expeller-pressed at controlled temperatures below 45°C to preserve natural nutrients and flavour.
4
Double Filtration
Two-stage filtration through food-grade ceramic filters. Crystal clear, clean oil — no sedimentation.
5
Lab Certification & Packing
FSSAI lab certification, nitrogen-flush packaging to prevent oxidation, then sealed and dispatched.

Best Practices for Cooking
1
Start with a Cold Pan
Pour Peanutji oil into a cold pan before turning on the heat. This helps the oil heat uniformly and prevents hot spots that can cause local burning.
Use a heavy-bottomed pan (cast iron or stainless steel) for most even results.
2
Heat to Shimmer — Not Smoke
Heat until the oil shimmers (around 160°C). You'll see gentle movement across the surface. For deep frying, test with a small piece of bread — it should sizzle gently and rise to the surface within 3 seconds.
3
Use for High-Heat Cooking
Groundnut oil's smoke point of ~232°C makes it ideal for deep frying, stir-frying, sautéing, and tadka. It won't break down or release harmful free radicals at normal cooking temperatures.
4
Perfect for Pickles & Marinades
The mild, nutty flavour pairs beautifully with Indian spices. Use unheated for pickling, salad dressings, or as a base for marinades — the raw oil retains maximum nutrients and Vitamin E.
5
Storage Tips
Store in a cool, dark place away from direct sunlight. Keep the bottle sealed tight. Avoid storing near the stove. Best consumed within 12 months of manufacture date printed on bottle.
Refrigeration is optional but extends shelf life in humid climates.
