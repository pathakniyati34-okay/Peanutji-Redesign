Our Story

Born from Saurashtra's
Rich Red Soil

"We didn't start Peanutji to make a product. We started it to make a promise — that what comes from our farms would reach your kitchen exactly as nature intended."

In 2014, two brothers from Rajkot walked into a processing facility and saw what the groundnut oil industry had become: chemical extraction, heavy refining, multiple adulterants, and no connection to the farmer who grew the peanut. They walked out with a simple idea — do it the right way, or don't do it at all.

Fishfa Agri World Private Limited was born from that conviction. Starting with a single cold-press unit in Rajkot, they built what is now one of India's most certified, most trusted, and most exported groundnut food brands.

Today, Peanutji ships to 25+ countries and five major cities across India. But the mission hasn't changed: Pyar Jaisa Pure.
